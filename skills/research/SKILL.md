# Research Workflow

1. Separate facts already known from claims that require current verification.
2. Prefer primary documentation and direct sources.
3. Cross-check consequential claims when another independent source is available.
4. Keep uncertainty explicit and cite the source title or URL used.
5. End with the decision-relevant conclusion rather than a dump of notes.
